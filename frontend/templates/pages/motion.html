How to read

ConceptMeaning
Visit One enter→exit traversal of the location zone. Visit Summary groups visits by event and enter-km cluster.
Enter / Exit Runner crosses into or out of the pin radius (from 5 s samples).
Window Midnight-aligned interval (default 5 minutes), shown as HH:MM.
Counts Unique runners entering or exiting in that window, by event (separate columns).
Context (km) Enter counts by visit cluster in that window (event enter_km:count). Keeps co-present streams visible (e.g. 10k@6.27 with full@22.7 = cross; 10k@6.27 with full@20.61 = parallel).
Movement drill-down Click a Visit summary row for that visit’s stats and concurrent partner streams (first / peak / last). Click a Passage window row for that window’s movement volumes and pace quintile profiles. Same-window concurrent volume is not a physical meeting. Optional cross/parallel/merge labels via package motion_movements.json.
TOTAL (Unique) Counts distinct runners who entered or exited the pin at least once, by event — not the sum of visit passages. Example: 700 10k runners each visiting twice → TOTAL (Unique) 700, Visit summary passages 1400.
