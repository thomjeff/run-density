Junction Reference

Definitions for Junction Flow analysis. Junctions are authored on the package; metrics are computed for the selected run.

Term Description
Junction A pin where course streams meet. Nearby segments are discovered from segment endpoints near the pin. Interactions (cross / merge) are declared by operators.
Cross One stream turns across another (From → To conflicts with a Conflicts stream). Crossing runners are on the turning path; crossed runners are on the conflict stream at the node.
Merge A joining stream enters one or more through (To) streams in the same direction. Joining runners arrive on From; through partners for counts are full and half on the To streams (same-event trail traffic does not unlock a merge count).
Node / dwell Presence is estimated at the junction endpoint (segment km at the pin) using gun time + start offset + pace × km. Each runner is treated as at the node for a short dwell (±15 s, 30 s total).
Co-presence / “met within 30s” A runner counts in the headline only if someone from the other stream is at the node within 30 s of their transit. Full or Half is enough — both events do not need to be present at once. One 10k meeting three Full runners still counts as one 10k.
Involvement window First to last minute where both roles have concurrent presence. Used for the timeline and “transited in window” context. Window traffic is larger than co-present uniques; do not read window counts as the headline.
Pace quintiles Event-relative field bands from runner pace: Q1 = lead (fastest) … Q5 = rear (slowest). Quintiles are computed within each event (a Q1 10k is among the fastest 10k runners, not the whole race).
Field crosstab Rows = crossing / joining runners’ own quintile. Columns = nearest co-present partner’s quintile (crossed / through). Cell = unique crossing/joining runners in that row whose nearest partner within dwell was in that column. Example: row Q5 × column Q1 ≈ rear of the crossing field meeting lead of the crossed field.
How to read Tiles are unique runners (a union — do not sum Full + Half to get the mixed total). Mix tables show event × event meetings; Unique matches the tile. The chart is concurrent occupancy over time, secondary to the unique mix.
